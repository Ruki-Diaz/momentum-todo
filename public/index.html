      Privacy & your data

      1. Trying Momentum without an account

      Your tasks, projects, tags and settings are saved in this browser on this device only. They aren’t sent to our servers, and they’re lost if you clear your browser data.

      2. With a free account

      • Signing in is handled by Clerk, a secure sign-in service.
      • Your tasks are stored in our database (hosted by Neon), so you can use them on any device.
      • Copying tasks from this device into your account doesn’t delete the copy on this device.
      • The AI assistant, when you use it, receives only the task details it needs to answer you.
      • The morning email, if you turn it on, is sent through Brevo, an email delivery service.

      3. Backups and deleting your account

      You can download a backup of your tasks at any time. If you delete your account, your account and everything saved in it are deleted from our database.

      AI assistant

      Meet your AI assistant

      It can plan your day, turn a goal into a list of tasks, and break big tasks into small steps. Here’s what it can and can’t see.

      What it sees

      • The titles and priorities of the tasks involved
      • Their due dates and project names
      • Simple totals, like how many tasks you finished

      What it never sees

      • Your email address or password
      • Your sign-in details
      • Tasks that aren’t needed for your request

      Requests are processed under OpenAI Zero Data Retention policies. Your tasks are never used to train AI models.
